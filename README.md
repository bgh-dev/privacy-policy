# Privacy Policy for Mood Match and FoodOpedia

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy covers our apps on the App Store: Mood Match and FoodOpedia.
We don't collect any data about you. The apps are free and show ads from
Google, and this policy explains how that works. If you have a question,
email us at [bgh.dev@icloud.com](mailto:bgh.dev@icloud.com).

## The short version

- **We collect nothing.** There are no accounts, no servers of ours and no
  analytics. We never receive information about you or about how you use our
  apps.
- **Ads come from Google.** They are personalized only if you allow tracking
  when iOS asks, and you can change your answer at any time.
- **Mood Match and Foodopedia keeps your app data on your device.** Your progress, settings and
   journals stay on your iPhone or iPad, and are never used for ads.

## Ads and tracking

Both apps show ads from Google AdMob. In Mood Match these are an ad between
some levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for extra moves or an
extra life.

**Tracking.** Before an app shows its first ads, iOS asks once whether you
allow it to track you. If you allow it, Google can use your device's
advertising identifier to show you ads that are more relevant to you and to
measure how they perform. If you don't, the apps still show ads, but Google
can't use that identifier, so the ads are less tailored to you. The apps work
the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in iOS
**Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

**What Google's ad software handles.** To deliver ads, it handles some
technical information when an ad is requested or shown: the device's IP
address, which gives a general location; basic information about the device
and the app; and whether an ad was seen or tapped. It also uses an identifier
limited to apps from the same developer to limit how often you see the same
ad, count ads and protect against fraud, and it may send diagnostic
information about its own software, such as crash reports. This information
goes to Google, not to us. Google explains how it uses it in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your ad choices.** Where the law requires consent, for example in the
European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent
form asks for your choices. In Mood Match it appears the first time an ad is
about to show, no ad is requested until you've answered, and you can review
or change your choices at any time in **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices**. The
form is provided by Google and lists the purposes its ad partners can ask
for.

## Mood Match

So that you can pick up where you left off, Mood Match saves these on your
device:

- your levels, scores, lives, achievements and daily challenges, and the
  emotions and pairings you have discovered
- a game in progress
- your settings, such as sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, and Calm or
  Timed mode
- your mood journal: the check-ins you make and any notes you add to them

None of this is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and none of it is
used for ads. Like other app data, it is included in your device's own
backups. It is deleted when you use **Settings → Reset Progress** in the
game, or when you delete the app.

The only time anything you saved leaves your device is when you choose to
export your mood journal. The game then opens the iOS share sheet with your
check-ins as a spreadsheet file, and the export goes only where you send it.

Mood Match is a game. Nothing you match, score or write
is used to assess your health or wellbeing. The game doesn't ask for your
camera, photos, contacts or location.

## FoodOpedia

FoodOpedia doesn't collect any personal data. It stores your preferences on your device for your exploration. The only information it
handles is what Google's ad software uses to show ads, described above.

## Sharing

We don't sell or share information about you, because we don't have any.
Apart from what Google's ad software handles to show ads, nothing leaves your
device unless you choose to share it yourself, such as an exported mood
journal.

## Audience

Our apps are made for a general audience. We don't collect information from anyone, children included. If
you have a concern, contact us.

## Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access,
correct or delete. You can remove everything an app keeps on your device by
deleting it, and in Mood Match also with **Settings → Reset Progress**. For
the information Google handles to show ads, see Google's Privacy Policy, or
contact us and we will help.
